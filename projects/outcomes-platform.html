Projects / Outcomes

An outcomes platform

Schedules every patient's assessments (PHQ-9, YOQ or a facility's own surveys) from their admit and discharge dates, then shows clinicians whether treatment is working.

Role
Designed, built and sold
Year
2022
Status
Live

The idea

Outcome measures only help if they actually get collected on time. The platform builds each patient's assessment schedule the moment they're admitted, chases the ones that are due, and charts the results so a clinician can see the trend instead of a stack of PDFs.
