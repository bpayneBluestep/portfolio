BlueIQ

BlueIQ is the brand behind BlueStep's AI initiatives. Baked directly into the tools that our users already have, we've built a series of features powered by HIPAA compliant LLMs to make charting and delivering care easier.

Role
Product owner, designer, engineer
Year
2025
Status
Live

What it does

Record with BlueIQ
Staff dictate in the browser, and the note comes back in the behavior, intervention and response structure insurers look for.
Patient Record Chatbot
Ask plain English questions about a patient and have your chatbot query through the patient's record, delivering answers and citing its sources along the way.
The daily milieu summary
Every morning leadership gets a plain-language recap of the previous day, helping them jumpstart on any problems they need to tackle.
Utilization review drafts
An AI powered narrative delivered to insurance companies, helping justify their levels of care. Sheds hours of time for clinicians from doing paperwork and fighting insurance companies.

Try it

The demo above is the same interface as the production feature, with an invented note.

Press Record with BlueIQ, then Record. No microphone is needed; the recording is simulated. When it stops, BlueIQ fills the Summary, Strengths and Concerns boxes from what was said.
