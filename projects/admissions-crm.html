The consultant CRM

Educational consultants are the people who place a struggling teenager in a program. They were running caseloads on spreadsheets and email. We built them a CRM on the same platform the programs use, so a referral can flow straight across.

Role
Product owner, designer, engineer
Year
2025 to 2026
Status
Live

What it does

Caseload
Clients, inquiries and alumni, each backed by a live record. A record is one place for the profile, files, contacts, communications and tasks.
Families
Contacts carry the roles that matter in this work: who decides, who signs, who pays.
Program directory
A shared library of treatment programs, searchable by level of care, population and geography. Nearly two hundred at last count.
Referrals
Send a client to a program and follow it from referred to admitted. When the program runs on BlueStep too, the referral arrives in their CRM.
Paperwork
The same e-signature and application builder from the program side, inside the consultant's workspace.
BlueIQ
An assistant that answers plain questions about a client or the whole caseload, citing the record.

How it was built

A TypeScript single-page app, hash-routed, with no framework. It deploys from GitHub as a static site on the BlueStep domain and talks to one endpoint, so the platform's session, permissions and data all come along for free.

Colors are OKLCH tokens with light and dark modes. Login is in-app, with a hand-off for two-factor. The whole thing type-checks as one program before it builds.
