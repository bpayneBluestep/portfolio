The referral marketplace

Educational consultants place families with programs, and programs live on those referrals. But everything between the two happens over phone calls and email.

Role
Product design
Year
2026
Status
In design

The idea

Each side tracks the relationship in its own CRM: who has a bed open, who fits this kid, how the last placement turned out. I'm designing the layer that connects them. The referral becomes a shared record both sides can see and act on, and openings and outcomes flow back so consultants can place families with real evidence.

The hard part is the same as in any two-sided product: each side needs its own view of the same truth, and neither should see what isn't theirs.
