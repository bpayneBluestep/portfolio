Projects / Platform

One product, many programs

BlueStep had been building every client's system by hand. Over twelve months we rebuilt it into a modular suite that ships ready to use and gets configured, not coded, for each program.

Role
Led the rebuild, wrote about half the code
Year
2025
Status
Live

How it works

Each program turns features on or off and sets up its own roles; one change to a role updates around fifty permissions for everyone who holds it. Changes are made once and rolled out by script to every customer, with a test customer first and a read-only check afterward that confirms each one landed.

Onboarding got faster, per-client engineering dropped, and the ceiling that had capped our growth for years went away.
